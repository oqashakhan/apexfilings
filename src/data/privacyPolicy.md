# APEX FILINGS — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

Apex Filings helps entrepreneurs establish and manage US businesses through company formation and related business services. This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Filings collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit https://apexfiling.com, submit a service request, create an account, make a payment, or communicate with our team.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will obtain it separately.

## 1. Information You Provide to Us

The information we collect depends on the service you request.

**US LLC Formation**

We may collect your proposed company name, business purpose, preferred state, owner or member details, mailing address, and other information required to prepare formation documents.

**EIN Assistance**

We may collect identity, ownership, business, and tax-related information required to prepare or support an application.

**Registered Agent and Business Address Services**

We may collect contact details, company information, address details, and documents needed to provide the requested service.

**Ongoing LLC Management**

For annual report filing, LLC amendments, registered agent changes, certificates of good standing, resale certificates, and LLC dissolution, we may collect existing company records, state registration details, ownership information, and filing instructions.

**Tax and Compliance Services**

For services including assistance relating to Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 for eligible foreign-owned US LLCs, we may collect relevant financial, ownership, identification, and tax information.

We may also collect your email address, phone number, billing details, account credentials, messages, uploaded files, and other information you choose to provide. Please submit only information and documents necessary for your requested service.

## 2. Information Collected When You Use Our Website

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, depending on the tools and configurations in use. This may include:

- IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
- Pages visited and interactions with website features.
- Referral source, visit time, and technical error information.
- Cookie preferences and other website usage data.

We use this information to maintain website functionality, understand how visitors use our services, improve performance, and help protect against misuse.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Apex Filings uses customer information to:

- Review service requests and determine what information is needed.
- Prepare, coordinate, and process business formation and compliance services.
- Communicate about missing documents, order progress, and completed work.
- Process payments and maintain transaction records.
- Provide customer support and respond to complaints or questions.
- Maintain customer accounts and make relevant documents available.
- Coordinate with government agencies and third-party service providers where necessary.
- Meet legal obligations and maintain appropriate business records.
- Protect our website, customers, and services against fraud or unauthorized access.
- Improve our services and send permitted business updates or marketing communications.

We will not knowingly use sensitive information for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

## 4. When We Share Customer Information

Some Apex Filings services require information to be provided to other organizations. We may share relevant information with:

**Government Agencies:** The appropriate US state filing authority or the Internal Revenue Service (IRS), when necessary for a requested filing or application.

**Service Partners:** Registered agent providers, business address providers, document processing partners, tax professionals, and other providers involved in delivering a requested service.

**Payment Providers:** Payment processors or financial service providers to handle transactions and related verification.

**Banks and Payment Platforms:** When you specifically request assistance with an application or authorize us to share information. Each provider makes its own eligibility and approval decisions.

**Technology Providers:** Website hosting, document storage, customer support, and other technical services used to operate our business.

**Legal Authorities:** When disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights and security.

We aim to share only information relevant to the purpose for which it is disclosed. Third-party organizations may process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

## 5. Customer Documents and Confidential Information

Customers may provide identity documents, company records, tax-related information, and other sensitive business documents. We use these documents to provide the requested services, communicate with relevant providers, and maintain records where necessary.

Access should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need the information for legitimate business purposes. Customers should avoid sending sensitive documents through public or unsecured channels where a designated submission method is available.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. International Customers and Cross-Border Processing

Apex Filings serves entrepreneurs and business owners who may live outside the United States, including customers in Pakistan and other countries.

Providing services to international customers may require information to be transferred to or processed in the United States or another country where a relevant service provider operates.

Privacy protections may differ between jurisdictions. Where required by applicable law, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 7. Payments and Financial Information

Payments for Apex Filings services may be handled through third-party payment providers.

Depending on the payment method, we may receive transaction confirmations, payment status, billing details, or limited payment-related information. Full payment card details may be handled directly by the payment provider rather than stored by Apex Filings.

Using a third-party payment service may also be subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

## 8. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to support core functionality, remember preferences, maintain security, and understand website performance.

Analytics or advertising technologies may also be used if configured on the website and permitted by applicable law.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any preference controls we provide. Blocking essential cookies may affect some website features.

Where legally required, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

## 9. How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for the period reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain customer records, resolve disputes, protect our business, and meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Retention periods vary depending on the service and type of record. Certain company formation, tax, payment, and compliance records may need to be retained for several years.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it, subject to legal retention requirements and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Measures may include access restrictions, secure systems, and appropriate safeguards for customer documents.

However, we cannot guarantee that our website, systems, or electronic communications will always be completely secure.
